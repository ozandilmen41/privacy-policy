# Privacy Policy for Wear OS Watch Faces

**Last Updated:** October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the **Terminal** watch face and all other Wear OS watch faces developed and published by the developer on the Google Play Store.

## 1. Data Collection and Use
We **do not** collect, store, share, or transmit any personal data, user information, or device identifiers to any external servers. Our watch faces operate entirely locally on your Wear OS smartwatch.

## 2. Health, Sensor, and Complication Data
Our watch faces may display health metrics (such as heart rate or step count), battery status, weather, or other data via standard Wear OS complications. 
* This information is read directly from your device's built-in system services solely for the purpose of displaying it on your watch screen in real time.
* We do not record, store, or transmit this data outside of your device.

## 3. Third-Party Services
Our watch faces do not contain third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking tools.

## 4. Children's Privacy
Our applications do not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly on this page.
